# User test: one procurement or legal-ops person, 15 minutes

Goal: one real reaction from someone who manages supplier contracts, quoted with their permission on Devpost before 21 December 2026. Never write the quote yourself, and never improve its wording beyond fixing a typo they agree to.

## Who to ask

Someone whose job includes supplier contracts: procurement, vendor management, legal operations, finance, or an IT manager who owns software renewals. One person is enough; two is better.

## Before the call

- Use `covenant-judge` (production), signed in as yourself. Share your screen; they don't need an Atlassian account.
- Open COVN-1 (Quillfeather) with the Covenant panel showing, and the Covenant register in a second tab.

## The script

1. **Their world (2 minutes).** Ask: "How do you keep track of notice deadlines and renewals today?" Write down what they say, word for word if you can.
2. **The contract (3 minutes).** Show COVN-1's renewal notice: the clause, its location, and **Why this date**. Ask: "Would you trust this date? What would you check first?"
3. **The decision (4 minutes).** Open **Renewal decision**: send-by dates, the drafted notice, the contract value, the supplier record. Ask: "Is anything here you'd normally have to dig for?"
4. **The portfolio (2 minutes).** Show the register's 90-day headline. Ask: "Would this change anything in your week?"
5. **Close (4 minutes).** Ask the three questions below, then ask for permission.

## Three questions

1. "What's the one thing in Covenant you'd use first?"
2. "What would stop you from using it?"
3. "In one or two sentences, how would you describe it to a colleague?"

## Permission

Read this out and note their answer: "May I quote what you just said on my hackathon page, with your job title and the kind of company you work for, but not your name?" If they say yes, read the quote back to them before you use it.
